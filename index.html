January–June

January · Build a repeatable week

One quality day, an easy spin, and 75–120 minutes of weekend endurance.

February · Get comfortable around two hours

Use a familiar long Spinervals session for selected 1¾–2¼ hour rides.

March · Ride outdoors and climb steadily

Build toward 2–3 hours when conditions allow. Add one sustained climb.

April · Practice repeat climbs

Build toward 3–4 hours on selected weekends. Try two sustained climbs when ready.

May · Practice climbing late in the ride

Work toward 4–5 hours on selected hilly rides. Practice fueling and pace the final climb.

June · Decide and rehearse

Review actual rides in early June. If the build went well, use a 5–7 hour hilly rehearsal scaled to the rides you’ve tolerated, then shorten the long rides ahead of July 10.
